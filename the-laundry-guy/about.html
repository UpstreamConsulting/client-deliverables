About

Advocating for the customer since 2004.

The Laundry Guy specializes in cost reduction for the uniform, linen, and mat rental sector — translating two decades of industry expertise into tangible savings on programs with Cintas, UniFirst, Vestis, and Alsco.

Darren Spenst saw that gap from the inside, as a former executive overseeing a $100 million division of the industry. With that insight, he pioneered a new category of cost reduction — one built entirely around the client's interests.

The company

Transform the industry — one invoice at a time.

The Laundry Guy was founded to give businesses auditing, renegotiation, and compliance monitoring built entirely around their interests — the support that helps them regain control of their laundry and uniform spend.

Since launching, the firm has helped companies of all sizes — from single-location shops to Fortune 500 enterprises — recover millions in overcharges and avoid future waste. What began as paper-based audits is now a fully digitized, software-driven model: automation, AI tools, and scalable processes that support organizations of any size.

The philosophy hasn't changed: cost reduction shouldn't be reactive or combative. It should be strategic, data-driven, and built on trust.

The team

Get to know the Laundry Guys.

Darren Spenst

Founder

Darren — the original Laundry Guy — brings more than 27 years of hands-on experience in the uniform, linen, and facility services industry. A former executive overseeing a $100 million division, he founded the firm to put that insider knowledge to work for the client. Today he guides the company's strategic direction and mentors its next generation of leadership.

Cardin Spenst

CEO

Cardin is the CEO and second-generation leader of The Laundry Guy. He led the firm's shift from paper-based audits to a fully digitized, software-driven model, and works with clients ranging from local dealerships to Fortune 500 enterprises — grounded in the belief that the work is about giving clients clarity, confidence, and control.
